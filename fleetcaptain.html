Treating Yacht Crew with Respect

We work with people, not data

A Heartfelt Farewell from Preferred Crew

After nearly 15 incredible years, the time has come for Preferred Crew to close this chapter and look ahead to new ventures. As we prepare to wind down operations, we want to take a moment to express our deepest gratitude to everyone who has been part of this journey.

To the exceptional crew members we've had the privilege of working with—thank you. Getting to know your stories, your ambitions, and your dreams has been one of the greatest honors of our work. If we’ve played even a small role in helping you move forward in your career, we consider that a true success.

To our valued clients and Captains—thank you for placing your trust in us. It has been a privilege to support you, your yachts, your teams, and your families. Being part of your onboard experience, even behind the scenes, has meant more than words can express.

To our peers, colleagues, and competitors in the wider yachting community—thank you for making this industry what it is: vibrant, resilient, and always evolving. We’ve learned so much from you, and we carry that with us into whatever comes next.

As promised from the very beginning, all the personal information shared with us will remain confidential. We are not selling any data, CVs, references, or contact information. Your trust has always been our most valued asset, and that will never change.

While this is goodbye for now, we hope to cross paths again—whether on the docks, at sea, or somewhere entirely new. On behalf of the entire team—Nelly, Jovana, Jasmin, Darko, Maria, Tiodor, and myself (Ian)—thank you for everything. It’s been an incredible ride.

If we had a soundtrack to play us out, we’d cue it now.

With sincere appreciation,

Ian and the Preferred Crew Team

P.S. We will not be sending any “final invoice” or “click here” messages. If you receive something of that nature, please don’t click—it’s not from us.
